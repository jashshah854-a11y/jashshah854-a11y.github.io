The gap is ballistic missiles.

On 6 July Russia launched 29 ballistic missiles and Ukraine intercepted none. In July it launched 126, and analysts count under a third downed. The interceptors that stop them are in short supply everywhere.

  • About 120 a month Ukrainian officials' estimate of Russian ballistic production S15
  • About 60 to 65 a month Patriot interceptors from Lockheed Martin, about 600 a year (Zelensky) S15
  • One third of the 2025 interceptor deliveries arrived in 2026 (Zelensky, 5 Aug) S16

Kyiv is being cut up, not only hit.

What to watch.

The evidence does not support the collapse of Ukraine's forces. It supports a hard winter by air. Weight the indicators yourself: this is a reasoning tool, not a forecast.
